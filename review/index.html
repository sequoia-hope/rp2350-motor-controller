2026-08-20

Warping Fields →

The rubber stretch failed on its algorithm, not its geometry: it blended the rigid-inclusion lag along each net's copper graph, which knows nothing about distance, so neighbouring traces on different nets sheared apart. One harmonic field per copper layer, evaluated by position, removes 92% of the DRC damage (465 → 37 at +1%); hand repair drops from 92 sites to 24. Side-by-side video of both engines, and a board-referenced map of the field itself — which is what caught the phase connectors being anchored 5.6 mm off their own pad centroid.

2026-08-25

Rubber Stretch & Violation Nudging →

The whole board dilated like a rubber sheet with the footprints as rigid inclusions: trace ends stay glued, airwires never move. The census proves F-46 — violating sites are nearly flat in k (68 at +0.25%, 91 at +1%), only the depth scales. A DRC-refereed nudger absorbs ~⅔ of the damage; the +1% board is staged for hand repair (92 sites, median 15 µm).

2026-08-24

GaN Power Stage Evaluation →

Four EPC23102 ePower Stage ICs instead of 8×BSC030N08NS5 + 4×EG3113: −32 parts and no gate loops, against stock-of-1 at LCSC, +$16–31/board, a 200 µs pulse limit that kills the fail-safe brake, and 19× the dV/dt into an unfiltered INA240. Verdict: not for rev-C — but it found two live findings in the stage we already ship.

Current top items

  • HIGH EG3113 gate driver is out of stock at LCSC (9 units, min buy 25; alternates at 0) and the board needs four — blocks ordering today, independent of any GaN question (F-40).
  • MED BSC030N08NS5 driven at Vdrive = 5.04 V, below its 6 V spec point: ~95 ns edges and an estimated ~2 W/bridge of switching loss. Vdrive can't just be raised — it feeds the TS5A3159s at 5.5 V max (F-41). Amended 2026-08-12 for the 20 A / 10 A current budget: the finding stands (20 A was always the analysis point), but the lead remedy is now a gate-resistor sweep, not a 10–12 V rail.
  • HIGH Encoder single-ended support: 100 Ω termination swamps the bias divider (±32 mV vs −100 mV threshold). Fix: switched termination on GPIO38/TP20 + P-side pull-ups (details). Field fix for built boards: R86–R93 → 560 Ω.
  • HIGH Phase TVS (SMAJ51A, 51 V standoff) contradicts the 60 V rating; bus TVS clamps above the 80 V FETs (details).
  • MED All analog-switch / transceiver enable lines float at boot — add pull-downs (details).
  • HIGH USB power path rescoped to rev-B for 5 A PD: LTC4359 + 100 V/4 mΩ FET replace the 65 V LM74700 (+100 mΩ Q5), U11 VBUS-pin rework + SMAJ24A before any >5 V contract (F-27/F-39).
  • MED Whole-board jiggle = 61% of tracks re-routed; do cluster-scoped shakes instead, starting with the encoder corner where the new switches land (details). 2026-08-20: measured at module granularity too — the board has no module-level slack; stretch is a re-route in disguise (modules page).
