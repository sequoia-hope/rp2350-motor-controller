Rev-B Schematic Changes — every delta against rev A

Netlist diff of the shipped schematic (tag rev-A-fab, c6b9b75) against the rev-B schematic on rev-b-lean (270af45 / bc35b15 — the same schematic as rev-b-fixes, plus the Tier A footprint re-points) · 2026-09-07 · both exported with kicad-cli sch export netlist and compared component by component, pin by pin.

In one paragraph. Rev B adds 33 components and removes one (291 → 323), changes 15 values, moves 17 existing parts onto different nets, and re-points 208 footprint fields. Every electrical change traces to a finding in the register: the switched encoder termination (F-01/02/03), the 64 V voltage philosophy on the TVS (F-09/10/11), the LED supply drop (F-16), the hardware E-stop option (F-17), CAN termination and barrier stitch (F-19), the 100 V ideal diode on the USB path and the USB ESD fix (F-27/F-39), and the 0402 audit (F-43). The footprint-only changes are Tier A of the space budget — and one of them, A1, the snubber shrink, is the change to take back: the sixteen snubber footprints have no value assigned and have been DNP since rev A, so the shrink was sized to a guess, and at 20 kHz on a 60 V bus a snubber above ~1.5 nF already exceeds an 0805 resistor's rating. Restoring the 0805 pads on the lean board costs nothing: they land exactly where rev A had them, with zero new DRC items (verified on the lean board and on the fresh-base fan candidate).

1 · The snubber decision (Tier A1 → revert)

Each of the four legs carries two RC snubbers: R17/C7 VMOT → /A_P, R18/C9 /A_P → GND, and likewise R37/C25 + R38/C26 on B, R48/C33 + R52/C34 on C, R80/C53 + R81/C54 on D. All sixteen parts have the placeholder values R and C, are DNP, have no LCSC field, and were empty pads on the shipped rev A. Tier A1 re-pointed the caps 0805 → 0402 and the resistors 0805 → 0603 on the assumption "1 nF/100 V X7R is a stock 0402 value", freeing 56 mm² in the half-bridge bands. Two things argue against it.

The resistor dissipates C·V²·fsw

Whatever the R value, a series-RC snubber charges and discharges its capacitor through the resistor every cycle and burns ½CV² each way. At the firmware's 20 kHz (driver->pwm_frequency = 20000) and a 60 V bus:

CP at 60 VP at 72 Vfits
470 pF34 mW49 mW0603 (100 mW)
1 nF72 mW104 mW0603 marginal · 0805 (125 mW)
2.2 nF158 mW228 mW1206 (250 mW)
4.7 nF338 mW487 mW1206 marginal · 2010

Steady-state at full PWM; the peak pulse power is far higher and is what limits thin-film 0603s first. An 0603 is also rated 75 V working voltage against a 60 V bus with ringing on top; 0805 is rated 150 V.

The capacitor wants voltage margin and C0G

A snubber cap sees the full bus plus overshoot on every edge, so it should be 100 V rated and, to keep the RC time constant stable, C0G/NP0 rather than X7R (X7R loses a large fraction of its capacitance under DC bias and drifts with temperature). In 100 V C0G the practical catalog ceilings are roughly 220 pF in 0402, 1 nF in 0603 and a few nF in 0805; X7R reaches ~1 nF in 0402 and ~10 nF in 0805. Shrinking to 0402 pins the design to "≤1 nF X7R" before the value has ever been chosen; 0805 keeps the whole plausible range (470 pF–4.7 nF) open on the same pads.

Verified: reverting is free on the fresh base. The lean board keeps the rev-A copper, so the sixteen parts sit at their rev-A coordinates with the small footprints centred on the old pads. Swapping them back to parts:C_0805_2012Metric_JLC / parts:R_0805_2012Metric_JLC (the A4 tight-courtyard variants) in a scratch copy and running kicad-cli DRC gives 0 courtyard or clearance items involving any snubber — on the lean board and on the fresh-base fan candidate (enc_fan_fresh). The cost is the 56 mm² the shrink would have freed, which nothing in the fan layout or the fresh-base plan needs (the encoder corner is x 112–131; the snubbers are at x 135–147). Recommendation: revert both the caps and the resistors to 0805, and if the chosen C ends up above ~1.5 nF, size the resistor pads 1206. Mechanically: change the A1 lines in hardware/tools/lean/edit_sch_footprints.py and re-run the footprint sync; the board side is a 16-footprint re-point.

4 · BOM readiness

27 fitted rev-B parts have no LCSC number. Six are not purchasable parts (H1–H4, JP1, JP2) and C57 was already blank on rev A. The real gaps, all from rev-B changes:

  • D5 SMDJ64A, D9 D13 D17 D21 SMAJ64A — marked LCSC-TBD-… (in F-26).
  • R101–R112 100 k 0402 — marked TBD (in F-26); trivial to fill.
  • R114 120 Ω 0603, R115 100 Ω 0603, C115 1 n/2 kV 1206 — blank, not listed in F-26; the 2 kV 1206 is the one that needs a real part pick.
  • Q6 is marked TBD but DNP.

Everything else new carries a number: U30–U33 C46388, R97–R100 C60490, C110–C114 C131394, D30 C2923942, D31 C169430, Q5 C29781176, U29 C688235, D1 C2890364.

5 · Things to decide or note

  1. A1 snubbers — revert to 0805 (§1). Both C and R; consider 1206 for R if the chosen C exceeds ~1.5 nF. Zero layout cost on the fresh base.
  2. Q6 E-stop vs reset brake. With Q6 fitted an open loop is a hard brake (F-12) with the firmware bus guard out of the loop. Fine if that is the intended E-stop behaviour on this bus; decide before populating.
  3. Channel D bias is connector-side. R100 pulls up /ENC_D_P at the connector while A–C pull up on the mux side; consistent with D having no mux (F-05), just different.
  4. Symbol names lag values on D5 (SMF54A symbol, SMDJ64A value) and D31 (SMAJ24A symbol, SMF24A value). Cosmetic; BOM must key on Value/LCSC.
  5. F-26 is incomplete: add R114, R115, C115 to the LCSC-pending list.
  6. Nothing here depends on the power-stage squeeze. Every schematic change places on the lean board's rev-A copper; the seven encoder-corner parts (U30–U33, R97, R98, R100, C110) are the ones the fan layout homes.
