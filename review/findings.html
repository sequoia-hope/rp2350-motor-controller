Amended 2026-08-12 — the board overrides the driver's own fail-safe. The EG3113 datasheet §2 gives HIN an internal 200 k pull-down and LIN̄ an internal pull-up to 5 V, so floating inputs park both power FETs off — the part ships both-off by design. Our external 10 k pull-downs sit on LIN̄ as well and win against that internal pull-up, converting both-off into low-side-ON. The brake state is therefore a deliberate board choice against the part's default, not something the driver imposed, which makes "consider both-off" cheaper than it looked: it needs only the four LIN̄ pulls turned into pull-ups. Do not simply remove them — the internal pull-up's impedance is not specified in the datasheet, so an explicit external pull-up is the defensible version. Source: hardware/docs/EG-Micro-EG3113_C480666_V1.2.pdf, found while retracting F-13
