F-41MED FETs driven ~5 V below their characterisation point: BSC030N08NS5 is specified 2.6/3.4 mΩ at VGS=10 V and 3.0/4.5 mΩ at 6 V with VGS(th) spanning 2.2–3.8 V, but Vdrive is 5.04 V. Amended 2026-08-12 — current budget rescoped; finding unchanged, remedy order changed. Targeting 20 A continuous with a 10 A shunt-desolder mode relaxes nothing above, because 20 A was already the analysis point: the schematic note has always read "20 Amp max continuous / 50 Amp peak absolute maximum", and the sense chain (2× 8 mΩ ∥ per phase → INA240A1 at 20 V/V → ±20.6 A FS; ±10.3 A desoldered) was scaled for 20 A all along — see power §1. What the rescope does open is a cheaper lever than a new rail: at 20 A the di/dt budget tolerates faster edges, and a gate-resistor sweep (10.2 → 5.1 → 2.2 Ω) reaches ~29 ns versus the 10 V rail's ~25 ns for zero BOM cost, recovering most of the 8 W. Revised order: (1) measure the edge, (2) gate-resistor sweep watching for dV/dt shoot-through, (3) FET respec to a VGS=4.5 V-characterised part at the next BOM touch — reopens F-11, (4) separate 10–12 V gate rail, demoted to last resort (it alone also fixes the RDS(on) half, ~2 W, but costs a whole regulator). In the 10 A mode F-41 is moot (~1.6 W/bridge total). Note for the thermal budget: the inline 4 mΩ shunts burn 4.8 W across A/B/C at 20 A — a bigger conduction term than F-41's ~0.2 W/phase
