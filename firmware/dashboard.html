Diagnosis: QSPI_SS (BOOTSEL) Pin Stuck LOW

The SWD debug probe confirms that QSPI_SS reads LOW despite the internal pull-up being enabled. Something external is pulling the pin to ground, causing the bootrom to enter BOOTSEL mode on every reset. The crystal, PLLs, flash, and firmware are all fine.

  • Measure QSPI_SS to GND with a multimeter — should be open when BOOTSEL button is not pressed
  • Inspect for solder bridges near the QFN-80 QSPI_SS pad and flash chip CS pin
  • If no BOOTSEL button exists on this board, add a 10kΩ pull-up from QSPI_SS to IOVDD
